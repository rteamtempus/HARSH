How to use this

A tour of everything that lives here. If something feels missing, check What's new — it's probably been built since.

Brain dump (home page)

The home page is one big text box plus a mic button. Type or say everything that's in your head; the assistant pulls out to-dos, events, routines, things to remember, even waste events ("we tossed the moldy bread"). It shows you a card per item to confirm before anything actually saves — tap Confirm all when the parse looks right or skip individual items.

It also answers questions: "is cheese on the grocery list?", "when's the dentist?". And it can ask follow-ups when something's ambiguous.

Lists

Make as many lists as you want — Grocery, Hardware Store, Books to Read. Default lists come pre-made. Brain dump routes new items to the right list automatically. Each item can carry optional fields: category, notes, deadline, lead time, priority. Deadline-aware items get smarter surfacing as their due date approaches.

Calendar

Shared events + connected external calendars. Both ICS feeds (any public iCal URL) and Google Calendar via OAuth are supported. Google sync is two-way: events you add in HARSH push to your primary Google calendar automatically. Manual sync via voice ("sync my calendars") or the Settings page; hourly auto-sync via pg_cron.

Routines surface on the calendar too — fixed-schedule routines (every Friday, etc.) show as dashed gold chips on their due days alongside events.

Routines

Recurring household obligations — trash, lawn, HVAC filter — that don't belong on a list and aren't events. Each routine has a cadence (interval like "every 7 days" or calendar like "every Friday") and surfaces in the briefing + calendar when it's due.

Actions: Done (advances the cycle + logs history), Snooze 1 day / 1 week, Skip this one (still advances), Pause (suspend until a date), Resume. Brain dump can create routines too: "trash goes out every Friday."

Briefings (on the display, and in the portal)

Calm partner voice updating you on what's today, what needs attention, what's coming. Hero line at the top, then sections for today's events, things needing attention, an ambient "right now" callout, and a heads-up section.

Daily, weekly, and monthly. Auto-regenerates on the schedule (6am / 12pm / 4:30pm / 7:30pm) plus when things change. Manual refresh from the Briefing page. If TTS is configured, the briefing gets a Play button — synthesized voice plays the spoken_text version.

Briefings respect quiet hours (default 9 pm – 7 am, configurable in Settings): only assertive items break through during the quiet window.

Weekly planning meeting

Sit down on Sunday evening, tap record, talk through the week. The app uploads the audio, transcribes via Gemini with speaker labels, then extracts a structured set of proposed to-dos, events, routines, household facts, context notes — and any open questions. Review everything on one screen, confirm or skip per item or per type, hit Save. The transcript stays; the audio is deleted after extraction.

Recipes

Snap a photo (handwritten, printed, magazine clipping — Gemini Vision reads them all). Tick Analyze with AI on save and the title auto-fills from the photo, ingredients come out structured, instructions land as a step block, and a rough cost estimate per ingredient + total gets computed.

Every field is editable. Tap Edit in the recipe lightbox to change the title, total cost, instructions, notes, or any ingredient (add/remove/change name/qty/unit/notes/cost). The photo stays as the source of truth; the extracted text is the queryable layer.

I made this / Threw out leftovers buttons on the recipe lightbox log a meal event tied to the recipe. The discard flow asks how many servings you actually ate and derives waste from the difference. Recent cooks (last 60 days) show under the recipe with their waste status.

Inventory & waste

Inventory tracks what's in the house, organized by category. Each category can hold multiple variants — "Soy sauce" with low-sodium, tamari, dark, etc. Items default to a boolean in/out flag; toilet paper and other count-based things can opt into quantity counts. Mark one variant as the category default so "we're out of soy sauce" without a specifier resolves correctly.

Waste is its own first-class thing. Capture it three ways:

  • Brain dump (passive) — say "we tossed the moldy bread" anywhere and it logs a waste event, not a list item.
  • Fridge cleanout dump on the Waste page — paste a stream of items ("the mustard was half-used, threw the lettuce") and it extracts each one with cost estimates and waste percentages.
  • Quick one-item form on the Waste page — pick item, reason, percentage, optional cost.

Reasons: spoiled, disliked, leftover not eaten, accident, not worth dealing with, other. The 7-day waste total shows at the top of the Waste page.

Household memory

Household facts are durable things you want the assistant to remember — "Daughter's pediatrician is Dr. X, last visit was…", "the trash bin is the green one." Categorized, searchable, used as context for briefings and queries.

Context notes are short-lived — max 30 days, auto-deleted after expiry. "Big presentation Friday — supportive tone." "Hard week, keep it gentle." They shape how the briefing reads, never auto-create tasks.

People & pets are non-login household members. Use them to attach routines or events to the kid, the dog, etc.

Settings

Family members, time zone, calendars (ICS + Google), assistant voice with audition (Chirp 3 HD voices), quiet hours, change password, Google Calendar connect/disconnect — all in Settings.

Voice everywhere

The mic button next to any text input transcribes you into that input. On the home page it streams into the brain dump. On the Waste page it streams into the fridge-cleanout dump. The display app has wake-word support ("Hey HARSH") and can switch calendar views or sync calendars by voice.

Inventory by voice (display). Say "we're out of soy sauce" and it marks the matched item out + adds it to the grocery list. Say "we just got bread" to put it back in stock. Say "do we have eggs?" to ask. The resolution ladder picks the right variant when there are multiple ("low-sodium soy sauce" vs "dark soy sauce"), and falls back to adding the bare phrase to groceries when nothing matches.

Waste + meals by voice. "We threw out the moldy bread" logs a waste event with a guessed reason and cost estimate. "I made lasagna" logs a meal event against the matched recipe. "We threw out the lasagna leftovers" closes the loop on the most recent cook within the last 7 days.
